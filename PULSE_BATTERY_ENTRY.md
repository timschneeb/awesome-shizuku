* [PULSE // BATTERY](https://github.com/kreza6173-pixel/pulse-battery) - Open-source Android battery utility with an overnight per-app alarm-wakeup report, verified one-tap standby restriction and revert, wake lock and alarm diagnostics, Doze controls, and APK backup. `MIT`

PULSE uses Shizuku and does not require root. It has no INTERNET permission, ads, or trackers. The project is tested on Xiaomi / Android 16 and the latest signed release is [v1.0.0](https://github.com/kreza6173-pixel/pulse-battery/releases/tag/v1.0.0).
